# Manifesto — sandala.dev

## Why this exists

Most software portfolios are résumés with screenshots. This one is different.

sandala.dev exists to embody a philosophy: that great software is an act of
thinking before it is an act of coding. That engineering quality is visible
through craft, not credentials. That a website — the first thing a prospective
client or employer sees — is itself a product, and products reveal their makers.

This site is the public expression of Abe Sandala's professional identity.
It is simultaneously a personal portfolio, an independent software consulting
platform, a lead-generation engine, and a knowledge publication. Its purpose
is to attract high-quality opportunities by demonstrating that the person
behind it understands both software engineering and the business outcomes
software exists to achieve.

## What it must feel like

Landing on sandala.dev should feel like meeting a person, not reading a brochure.
The design, motion, and copy should communicate taste, confidence, and precision —
qualities that are difficult to fake and impossible to separate from the work.

The UI is the argument. A world-class interface built with intentional motion,
careful typography, and deliberate hierarchy says more than any claim about
quality ever could.

## What it must never become

- A generic freelancer portfolio that lists technologies
- A template agency site optimised for volume
- A corporate consultancy pretending to have a large team
- A personal diary with no commercial focus
- A buzzword showcase that mistakes feature lists for positioning

## The guiding promise

Every decision — design, engineering, copy — should reinforce one idea:
*this is what thoughtful software made by someone who cares looks like.*
