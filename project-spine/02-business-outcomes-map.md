# Business Outcomes Map — sandala.dev

Every piece of work on this project must advance at least one of the outcomes
below. If a task cannot be traced to an outcome, it is scope creep.

## Outcome 1 — Generate qualified consulting inquiries

**Why:** Revenue depends on attracting businesses that value long-term software
partnerships, not the cheapest website quote.

**How the site advances it:**
- Services section communicates the full value ladder (websites → integrations
  → business systems → AI → long-term consulting)
- Case studies and work samples demonstrate business impact, not just technical
  execution
- Contact form is prominent, frictionless, and delivers to Resend immediately
- Positioning language speaks to decision-makers, not developers

**Signal of success:** Inbound messages from businesses describing a real problem
and a budget. Quality over volume.

## Outcome 2 — Attract senior technical opportunities

**Why:** Employer interest (CTOs, engineering managers, remote companies)
diversifies income and validates positioning.

**How the site advances it:**
- Technical depth is visible in the engineering choices (Next.js, TypeScript,
  Framer Motion, GSAP, R2) — the site *is* the demo
- Architecture and design decisions are defensible and explained
- Blog/essays demonstrate thinking, not just doing
- GitHub and LinkedIn are clearly signposted

**Signal of success:** Inbound interest from technical employers describing
senior-level roles.

## Outcome 3 — Establish ongoing reputation through content

**Why:** Writing builds compound interest. Developers and founders who return for
content become referrers and future clients.

**How the site advances it:**
- Blog is first-class, not an afterthought
- Posts are thoughtful, specific, and useful — not SEO filler
- Content strategy ensures consistent voice and proof hierarchy

**Signal of success:** Returning visitors; newsletter/RSS subscribers; referral
traffic from shared articles.

## Outcome 4 — Demonstrate design and engineering excellence through the site itself

**Why:** A portfolio that looks average cannot credibly claim to deliver exceptional
work. The site is the primary proof point.

**How the site advances it:**
- World-class UI: Tailwind + shadcn/ui + 21st.dev elements + Lottie + Framer Motion
  + GSAP — used with intentionality, not decoration
- Core Web Vitals green; animations never degrade usability
- Mobile-first, accessible, fast globally via Cloudflare R2 CDN edge

**Signal of success:** The UI earns unsolicited comments about quality from
visitors and peers.
