# Charter

## One job

sandala.dev turns Abe Sandala's work, judgment, and point of view into credible
evidence that helps the right clients and technical teams start a conversation.

## Product position

The site occupies the space between a personal brand, boutique consultancy,
product studio, and technical publication. It must feel like meeting the person
behind the work, not reading a generic freelancer brochure.

## Audiences

- Businesses, founders, agencies, and operators seeking a long-term software
  partner.
- CTOs, engineering managers, and product teams evaluating senior technical
  judgment.
- Developers, designers, and founders who discover Abe through useful work and
  writing.

## Scope and constraints

The portfolio is a Next.js and TypeScript application, static by default, with
Tailwind styling and purposeful Framer Motion, GSAP, or Lottie usage. It has no
database, CMS, or authentication. Scrumtrulescent Magazine is independent; this
site consumes selected magazine content read-only and must degrade gracefully.
Accessibility, reduced motion, responsive behavior, performance, and honest
public claims are part of the product.

Detailed design, content, data, risk, and technical references remain under
`project-spine/00-original-intent.md` through
`project-spine/12-ui-element-map.md`. They are preserved evidence, while this
file is the lean briefing source for future sessions.

## Human authority

Abe owns product intent, public voice, unresolved personal copy, launch claims,
and final review. Agents may propose and implement bounded work, but do not
invent client outcomes, credentials, pricing, or personal beliefs.
