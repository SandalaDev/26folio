# Project Brief

## 1. What are we building?

sandala.dev is Abe Sandala's public professional home: a flagship portfolio,
consulting platform, and proof of engineering and product judgment. Its primary
job is to help serious clients and technical leaders understand how Abe thinks,
what he can build, and why he is a credible long-term engineering partner.

## 2. Why now? / Why this?

The site must convert years of design, engineering, systems, and business
experience into a coherent public position. Generic portfolio conventions
undervalue that experience. The product itself must demonstrate the quality,
care, and strategic thinking it claims.

## 3. Scope (in / out)

In scope: the static sandala.dev portfolio, consulting positioning, work and
capability proof, personal narrative, contact path, and a read-only connection
to Scrumtrulescent Magazine. Out of scope: a database, CMS, authentication,
payments, a local blog, low-cost agency positioning, and pretending to be a
large consultancy. Scrumtrulescent remains a separate project and codebase.

## 4. Constraints

Use Next.js App Router, TypeScript strict mode, Tailwind CSS, accessible
component composition, and purposeful motion. The site remains static by
default and deploys through Dokploy. Secrets stay in environment variables.
Performance, accessibility, responsive behavior, reduced-motion support, and
authentic public language are non-negotiable. Detailed legacy constraints remain
in `project-spine/06-project-technical-plan.md` and
`project-spine/07-architecture-principles.md`.

## 5. Success looks like

The site generates qualified consulting inquiries, attracts senior technical
opportunities, strengthens reputation through useful content, and earns trust
through its own design and engineering quality. Delivery progress is estimated
from linked work; actual outcomes require real inquiries, opportunities,
returning readers, performance measurements, and human feedback.

## 6. Open questions

Launch timing, final deployment configuration, live contact delivery, missing
portfolio assets, real social links, and several owner reviews remain open.
Current details are maintained in `project-state/completion.md`.
