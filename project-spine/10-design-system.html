§3

Colour guardrails

Hard anti-patterns — rules, not preferences

✕ No magenta

rose stays dusty / coral. If a pink drifts toward #ff00ff hot fuchsia, it's wrong. Moderate saturation.

✕ No harsh gradients

Transitions between rose, peach, caramel are long and low-contrast — soft washes, large blur radii. Never a tight two-stop ramp.

✕ No pure black, no cold navy

The base is always warm dark.

✓ Accents are seasoning

Large surfaces stay base tones. Rose and caramel punctuate — they are not the meal.
